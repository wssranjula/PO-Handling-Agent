blue-therm16, blue thermal bottle, insulated drink bottle blue, or blue bottle 16oz.

lid spare, replacement bottle lid, spare thermos lid, and replacement lids for insulated bottles.

MUG-WHT, white mug, ceramic mug white, and white ceramic mug retail boxed.
